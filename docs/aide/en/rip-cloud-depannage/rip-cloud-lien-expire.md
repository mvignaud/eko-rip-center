Links sent by e-mail have a short life and work only once.
An "invalid" link almost always comes down to one of these two cases.

## Before you start

- Find the latest e-mail received: only the most recent one counts.
- You need access to the account's mailbox.

## Steps

1. For an **address confirmation link** (valid for 24 hours):
   1. Open the portal and sign in.
   2. In the **Confirm your e-mail address** banner (*Confirmez votre adresse e-mail*), click **Resend the e-mail** (*Renvoyer l'e-mail*).
   3. Open the new e-mail, click the link, then the button on the page.
2. For a **forgotten-password link** (valid for 1 hour):
   1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?** (*Mot de passe oublié ?*).
   2. Enter the account's address and click **Send the link** (*Envoyer le lien*).
   3. Open the new e-mail within the hour and choose a new password.

## Good to know

- A link that has already been used no longer works.
- For confirmation, merely opening the link uses nothing up: it is clicking the button on the page that validates.
- You can request at most 3 e-mails per hour.
- If nothing arrives, see [Fix e-mails that do not arrive](rip-cloud-emails-non-recus.md).
