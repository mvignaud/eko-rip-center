The platform's e-mails come from **E-KO RIP Cloud**.
A few checks are usually enough.

## Before you start

- Confirmation, forgotten-password and alert e-mails use your account's address.

## Steps

1. Look in the spam folder.
2. Open **My account** (*Mon compte*) and check the e-mail address.
3. Check the **Receive alerts** and **Receive the weekly summary** boxes (*Recevoir les alertes*, *Recevoir le résumé hebdomadaire*).
4. If it is a confirmation e-mail, click **Resend the e-mail** (*Renvoyer l'e-mail*) in the banner.

## Good to know

- The e-mails come from `no-reply@e-ko.fr`: add it to your trusted senders if needed.
- Do not reply to these messages: they are sent from an address that does not receive mail.
- The "new app connected" e-mail is always sent.
- Live support replies arrive by e-mail when the page is closed.
