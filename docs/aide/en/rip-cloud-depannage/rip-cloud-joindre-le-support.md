Live support puts you in touch with the team from the portal.
You have no other account to create.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the portal.
- The blue live support bubble appears at the bottom right of every page.

## Steps

1. Click the blue bubble at the bottom right, or **Support** in the sidebar.
2. Write your message and send it. You can attach a screenshot or a log.
3. Read the team's replies in the bubble.

![Portal Support page with the live support bubble at the bottom right](../../images/cloud-support-bulle.png)

## Good to know

- No need to introduce yourself: your name, e-mail and company come with the message.
- Closing the bubble does not end the conversation. If the page is closed when the team replies, the reply arrives by e-mail.
- When you sign out of the portal, the conversation is removed from this browser.
- Old requests (`TKT-…`) remain readable for 30 days on the **Support** page: quote their reference in the bubble. See [Find an old support request](rip-cloud-ancienne-demande-de-support.md).
- The team sees which account the message comes from. The history of your conversations stays in the bubble.
- If the bubble is not available, the **Support** page points to contact@e-ko.rip.
