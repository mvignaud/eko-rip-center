The live support bubble only appears on portal pages, once you are signed in.
A browser extension can hide it.

## Before you start

- Check that you are signed in to the portal.

## Steps

1. Open the settings of your ad blocker or privacy extension.
2. Allow `platform.e-ko.rip`.
3. Reload the page.

![Portal Support page with the bubble at the bottom right](../../images/cloud-support-bulle.png)

## Good to know

- The **Support** link in the sidebar opens the same bubble.
- Otherwise, write to contact@e-ko.rip.
- The bubble is only present on portal pages, once you are signed in.
