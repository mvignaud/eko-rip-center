The platform warns you when backups run out of space or stop arriving.
Here is how to react.

## Before you start

- Space alerts go to the owner.
- The failure alert goes to the site's owner and administrators.

## Steps

1. If you receive a space alert (80% or 100%), open **Subscription** (*Abonnement*) to see usage.
2. Subscribe to a plan with more storage, or free up space.
3. If you receive a failure alert, check that the server is switched on and connected to the Internet.
4. Open **System › E-KO RIP Cloud** (*Système › Cloud E-KO RIP*) to see the connection status.

## Good to know

- At 100%, new backups are refused until space is freed or the plan changes.
- The failure alert is sent if no nightly backup arrives for two nights.
- The space alert arrives at 80%, then at 100% of the plan's storage use.
- The weekly summary repeats the backup status of each site.
- These alerts are independent of the e-mail boxes in **My account** (*Mon compte*).
