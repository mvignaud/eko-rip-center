With the Pro plan, you can apply one site's backup to another of your sites.
It is handy for equipping a new workshop with the configuration of an existing one.

## Before you start

- The account owner must have the **Pro** plan.
- Both sites must belong to the same owner, and you must be the owner or an administrator of them.
- The restore replaces the target site's configuration.

## Steps

1. Open **My sites** (*Mes sites*), then click **Backups** (*Sauvegardes*) under the source site.
2. Find the version you want.
3. On its line, choose the target site in the drop-down list: **This site** (*Ce site*), or the name of the other site.
4. Tick **Replace the current settings** (*Remplacer les réglages actuels*).
5. Click **Restore** (*Restaurer*), then confirm in the window that opens.

   ![A site's Backups page: each version offers Download, the Replace the current settings box and the Restore button](../../images/cloud-sauvegardes.png)

## Good to know

- The list of target sites only appears with the Pro plan, when you have at least one other site.
- Without the Pro plan, the message "La restauration vers un autre site est réservée à l'offre Pro, entre sites du même propriétaire" appears (restoring to another site is reserved for the Pro plan, between sites of the same owner).
- The target site must be online to receive the order.
- A "Backup restored" e-mail warns the owner and the administrators.
