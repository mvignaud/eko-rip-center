Card payment happens on the page, without leaving it.
The platform never sees your card number.

## Before you start

- You have just clicked **Continue to payment** (*Continuer vers le paiement*): see [Subscribe to a paid plan](rip-cloud-souscrire-une-offre.md).
- Have your card at hand and, if your bank requires it, your app or phone to confirm.

## Steps

1. On the **Payment** page (*Paiement*), check the reminder of the plan and billing period.
2. Click **Bank card** (*Carte bancaire*) if that tab is not already active.
3. Enter the card in the secure field of the **Bank card** section.
4. Click **Save the card and confirm** (*Enregistrer la carte et valider*).
5. If your bank asks for confirmation (3-D Secure), it appears in a window above the page: confirm it.

The page then confirms that the subscription is active.

## Good to know

- The card is entered in a secure field of the payment provider, not on the platform.
- For the Atelier plan as a first subscription, the card is only checked during the trial: no charge before the 30 days end. See [Make the most of the Atelier plan's 30-day trial](rip-cloud-essai-atelier.md).
- Card payment needs JavaScript in the browser; otherwise, choose SEPA direct debit.
- To change card later, click **Change payment method** (*Changer de moyen de paiement*) in **Subscription**. See [Change plan, payment method or cancel](rip-cloud-changer-ou-resilier.md).
- If the payment is declined, see [What to do when a payment is declined](rip-cloud-paiement-refuse.md).
