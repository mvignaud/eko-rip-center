The **Subscription** (*Abonnement*) page brings together every possible change.
You stay in control at all times.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the portal with the account that subscribed.
- Know what each action changes: see "Good to know".

## Steps

1. Open **Subscription**.
2. To change plan, click **Change plan** (*Changer d'offre*), choose the new plan and confirm.
3. To change billing period, click **Switch to annual billing** (*Passer en facturation annuel*) or **monthly**: the price of the new period is shown next to it.
4. To change payment method, click **Change payment method** (*Changer de moyen de paiement*), then enter a new card or create a new mandate.
5. To stop, tick **I cancel at the end of the current period** (*Je résilie à la fin de la période en cours*), then click **Cancel** (*Résilier*).

   ![Subscription page with the current plan, the Usage section and the change buttons](../../images/cloud-abonnement.png)

## Good to know

- A change of plan or billing period is immediate: a new period starts at the price of the new plan.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, which is not refunded. The account then returns to the Free plan.
- If a payment fails and is not settled, the subscription is cancelled and the account returns to the Free plan.
- Check that your fleet fits within the Free plan before cancelling.
- After a scheduled cancellation, the page shows "Résiliation programmée à la fin de la période" (cancellation scheduled at the end of the period).
- With an Atelier subscription in its trial, cancelling before the end avoids any invoice. See [Make the most of the Atelier plan's 30-day trial](rip-cloud-essai-atelier.md).
- When the account returns to the Free plan, existing backup versions are kept for 90 days, then only the latest one is kept.
- For a declined payment, see [What to do when a payment is declined](rip-cloud-paiement-refuse.md).
