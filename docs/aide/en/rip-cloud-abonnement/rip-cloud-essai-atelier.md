You can try the Atelier plan for 30 days without paying.
A card is only checked, not charged.

## Before you start

- The trial applies only to your account's **first paid subscription**, and only if it is the **Atelier** plan.
- The trial is offered once per account, even after a cancellation.
- It is shown when you subscribe.

## Steps

1. Open **Subscription** (*Abonnement*), then **See the plans** (*Voir les offres*).
2. Choose the **Atelier** plan and click **Choose** (*Choisir*).
3. Fill in the company, the billing contact and the payment method. See [Subscribe to a paid plan](rip-cloud-souscrire-une-offre.md).
4. Confirm the payment: the card is checked, with no charge.
5. On **Subscription**, find the note "Essai gratuit jusqu'au" (free trial until) followed by the end date.

## Good to know

- No invoice is issued before the trial ends.
- An e-mail warns you that the trial is ending.
- An e-mail confirms the subscription, then each invoice issued.
- To avoid being billed, cancel before the trial ends: see [Change plan, payment method or cancel](rip-cloud-changer-ou-resilier.md).
- The subscription status shows as **Trial in progress** (*Essai en cours*), then **Active** (*Actif*).
