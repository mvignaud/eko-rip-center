When a payment is declined, you are warned and can settle it by changing your payment method.
If you do nothing, the account returns to the Free plan.

## Before you start

- An e-mail warns you right away of the decline.
- Sign in to the portal with the account that subscribed.

## Steps

1. Read the e-mail: it offers a **Update my payment method** button (*Mettre à jour mon moyen de paiement*).
2. Open **Subscription** (*Abonnement*).
3. Click **Change payment method** (*Changer de moyen de paiement*).
4. Enter a new card or create a new mandate. See [Pay by bank card](rip-cloud-payer-par-carte.md) or [Pay by SEPA direct debit](rip-cloud-payer-par-sepa.md).
5. Check that the status no longer shows **Payment overdue** (*Paiement en retard*).

## Good to know

- The platform makes new attempts, with reminders around day 3, day 7 and day 14.
- If not settled, the subscription is cancelled and the account returns to the Free plan; an e-mail says so.
- After that return, existing backup versions are kept for 90 days, then only the latest one is kept.
- If your fleet then exceeds the Free plan, a 14-day grace period opens. See [Bring a fleet that exceeds the plan back in line](rip-cloud-parc-depasse-l-offre.md).
