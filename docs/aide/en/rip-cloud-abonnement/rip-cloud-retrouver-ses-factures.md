Each invoice is available as a PDF on the portal.
You also receive it by e-mail.

## Before you start

- You need a paid plan.
- Sign in to the portal with the account that subscribed.

## Steps

1. Open **Subscription** (*Abonnement*).
2. Find the **Invoices** section (*Factures*): the table shows the **Period** (*Période*), the **Status** (*État*), the **Amount** (*Montant*) and the **Invoice** (*Facture*).
3. Download the invoice you want as a PDF.

## Good to know

- An e-mail confirms the subscription, then each invoice. It goes to the billing address given when subscribing.
- The **Subscription** page also shows **usage**: machines and sites counted, backup space used.
- If a payment fails, an e-mail says so and new attempts are made. See [What to do when a payment is declined](rip-cloud-paiement-refuse.md).
- Invoices are issued by **GOLDEN PRINT**, at the start of each period. During the Atelier plan trial, no invoice is issued.
- Before the first invoice, the section shows "Aucune facture pour l'instant." (no invoice yet).
- The signed SEPA mandate remains downloadable from the **Subscription** page.
