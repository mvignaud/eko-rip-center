When you have more sites or machines than your plan includes, the cloud warns you.
You have 14 days to put things right.

## Before you start

- An e-mail warns the owner and the administrators of the sites concerned.
- Open **Subscription** (*Abonnement*) to see usage and the machines counted site by site.

## Steps

1. Open **Subscription**.
2. Check the machines and sites counted.
3. Subscribe to a suitable plan with **See the plans** (*Voir les offres*), or change plan.
4. Or remove the extra machines or sites.

## Good to know

- If nothing is done within 14 days, **remote access** to the sites concerned is **suspended**.
- Use in the workshop is never affected: the server keeps printing and cutting.
- As soon as things are put right, remote access comes back.
- The owner and administrators are warned by e-mail, by notification and by a banner on **My sites** (*Mes sites*) and **Subscription**.
- Once access is suspended, remote requests get the response "plan exceeded" (code 402), and the browser shows an explanatory page.
- See how machines are counted: [Understand the machines counted in your plan](rip-cloud-comprendre-les-machines-comptees.md).
