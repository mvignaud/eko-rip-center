The **E-KO RIP** app lets you follow the workshop from a phone: print queue, machine status, ink levels and alerts. It warns you as soon as a machine stops with an error, even when the phone is in your pocket.

## Before you start

You have an iPhone or an Android phone. No E-KO account is needed to install the app: it connects only to the workshop's RIP server. Allow 2 minutes.

## Steps

1. Open https://e-ko.rip/app/ on your phone.
2. Follow the download link for your system (App Store or Google Play).
3. Install the app, then open it.
4. Connect it to your server (see [Connect the mobile app to the workshop server](rip-connecter-l-application-mobile-a-l-atelier.md)).

## Good to know

- The app is for **monitoring**. It sends no files and sets neither printing nor cutting: those operations are done from a workstation.
- What it lets you do: see each machine's queue, start, pause, stop, retry or move an output to the front, check machine status, check the inks and the remaining media length, read the alert history and receive notifications.
- A question or a problem? Write to contact@e-ko.rip.
- News and availability on the app stores are announced on the app page.
- To explore it without a server, see [Try the mobile app in demo mode](rip-essayer-l-application-mobile-en-mode-demo.md).
