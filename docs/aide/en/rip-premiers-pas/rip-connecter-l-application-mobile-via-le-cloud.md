The cloud lets you follow the workshop from outside, with no VPN. The app opens the platform in the phone's browser, then lists your sites.

## Before you start

The server is linked to the platform (see [Link the server to the Cloud](../rip-cloud-premiers-pas/rip-cloud-relier-un-serveur.md)) and you have a platform account. Allow 2 minutes.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Connect to the cloud** (*Se connecter au cloud*), under the "hors de l'atelier" (away from the workshop) line. The phone's browser opens on the platform.
2. Sign in with the platform account, then authorise the app.
3. The app opens on the **Your sites** (*Vos sites*) screen, which lists the account's sites, online or not.
4. Tap the site you want. No second password is asked.

## Good to know

- Another way: enter the site's remote address in **Server** (*Serveur*), in the form `https://<site>.platform.e-ko.rip`, then an account of the RIP server. Entering `platform.e-ko.rip` in **Server** is the same as tapping **Connect to the cloud**.
- The role shown in the app comes from the one given to your account on the site. Access stays valid for about 180 days.
- To sign out, tap **Sign out of the cloud** (*Se déconnecter du cloud*) on the **Your sites** screen. You can also disconnect apps from **My account** (*Mon compte*) on the portal (see [Disconnect an app from the cloud](../rip-cloud-compte/rip-cloud-deconnecter-les-apps.md)).
- Right after the first sign-in, the app asks for permission to send notifications. It then also receives those of the platform: site offline, site back online, new app connected to the account. They follow the **Receive alerts** (*Recevoir les alertes*) checkbox in **My account**.
- A site shown as "hors ligne" (offline): see [A site appears offline in the mobile app](../rip-depannage/rip-site-hors-ligne-dans-l-application-mobile.md).
