Demo mode shows the app with sample data, with no server and no connection. Use it to explore the app before linking it to the workshop.

## Before you start

The app is installed. No connection is needed.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Try demo mode** (*Essayer le mode démo*).
2. Browse the **Queue** (*File*), **Machines** (*Machines*), **Alerts** (*Alertes*) and **Settings** (*Réglages*) tabs.
3. To leave, open **Settings** and tap **Leave demo mode** (*Quitter le mode démo*).

## Good to know

- Nothing is saved and nothing is sent.
- Notifications are not available in demo mode.
