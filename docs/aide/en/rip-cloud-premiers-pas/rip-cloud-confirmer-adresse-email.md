Confirming your e-mail address is required to link a server.
It applies to every account created with a password.

## Before you start

- You have created a platform account.
- The confirmation link is valid for 24 hours.

## Steps

1. Open the e-mail sent by **E-KO RIP Cloud**.
2. Click the confirmation link.
3. Click the button on the page to confirm.

## Good to know

- Until the address is confirmed, a banner says so on **My sites** (*Mes sites*) and **My account** (*Mon compte*). It offers **Resend the e-mail** (*Renvoyer l'e-mail*).
- Linking a server is refused until the address is confirmed. The rest of the portal works.
- If the e-mail does not arrive, look in the spam folder, then ask for a new one from the banner.
- An account created with Google is confirmed automatically, as are older accounts.
- Merely opening the link confirms nothing: it is clicking the button on the page that validates.
- You can request at most 3 resends per hour.
- If the link has expired, click **Resend the e-mail**: see [Fix an expired or invalid link](../rip-cloud-depannage/rip-cloud-lien-expire.md).
