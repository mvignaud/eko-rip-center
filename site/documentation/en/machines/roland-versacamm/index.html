    Roland VersaCAMM VP-300 / VP-540

    Connect the Roland VersaCAMM and send it the cut over the network.

    #What the RIP does

    The Roland VersaCAMM is a printer/cutter. The RIP sends it the cut over the network (roll width 762 or 1371 mm). Printing through the Roland is not driven by the RIP yet.

    Note

    Support for the Roland is recent: report anything unexpected with Report a problem… (Signaler un problème…) in the client.

    #Connect

    Network, fixed IP, Network (TCP) connection.

    #Check

    nc -vz <ip> <port> from the server.
