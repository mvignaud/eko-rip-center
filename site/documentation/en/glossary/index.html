    Glossary

    RIP and wide-format printing terms, explained simply.

    The words of the screen and of the documentation, with their French equivalent (the one on the French screen and in the French documentation). Each feature is described in usage.

    #Jobs and queue

    TermIn FrenchDefinition
    JobTravailOne received file (PDF, SVG, TIFF, PNG, JPEG) with its request: settings, pages, rendered plates, ticket.
    RenderingRenduTurning the file into ink plates (Ghostscript), done once on arrival; a print or a cut goes out again without rendering again.
    PreflightPré-vérificationCheck on arrival: fonts not embedded, low-resolution images, cut paths, sizes. It warns, or refuses when the preset says so.
    PresetRéglageA named set of choices (resolution, media, white, cutting, registration…), applied when sending or by a hot folder.
    Hot folderDossier surveilléServer folder where every dropped file becomes a job, with the folder's preset (entrees/<preset>/).
    RuleRègleIn entrees-auto/, picks a file's preset from its name or page size.
    Tagged name patternMotif à balisesIn entrees-auto/, shape of the file name ({nom}__{machine}__{reglage}__{copies}) giving preset, machine, copies…
    Job ticketFiche travailA job's order number, customer, company, quantity, due date, notes and operator.
    OutputSortieOne page of a job sent to a machine (print or cut), in the queue.
    QueueFile d'attenteThe outputs still to run, machine by machine, in order; a machine runs one output at a time.
    Waiting reasonRaison d'attenteWhy an output does not go (machine unreachable, other media, too wide…); the next ones go ahead of it.
    SpoolTamponSize of the files the outputs still to run will send to the machines.
    HistoryHistoriqueFinished, failed or cancelled outputs; Redo runs one again without rendering.
    ProofBAT (bon à tirer)A copy to approve; on a second machine, the "-BAT" copy goes and the original waits, held.
    PoolPoolIdentical machines grouped under one name: the output goes to the first one ready.
    PurgePurgeDeleting old inactive jobs, after the retention time or below the free space threshold.

    #Ink and colour

    TermIn FrenchDefinition
    PlatePlaque8-bit image of one ink (0 = full ink, 255 = no ink).
    Spot colourTon directNamed colour in the PDF (White, Silver, CutContour…), output on its own plate instead of being mixed into CMYK.
    Cut spot colourTon de découpeSpot colour carrying the cut paths (CutContour…); never printed.
    Spot colour tableTable des tons directsExact name, aliases and Lab colour of each spot colour, to convert or recognise it.
    White / Silver (metallic)Blanc / ArgentThe Epson's special inks, recognised by the spot colour name (White, Metallic…).
    UnderlaySous-coucheWhite laid under the ink, for clear or coloured media.
    ChokeRétractionWhite pulled back from the ink edge, so it does not show past the colour.
    ICC profileProfil ICCColour description of a media or a colour space, uploaded to the server.
    Rendering intentIntention de renduHow an out-of-gamut colour is brought into the media's gamut.
    Print modeMode d'impressionResolution, passes, heating, light-ink switch and total ink of a media.
    Light-ink switchBascule des clairesWhere the dark ink takes over from the light one (Lc, Lm, Lk): soft, balanced, sharp.
    Total ink limitEncre totale / Limite d'encreMaximum sum of the inks at one point, in %.
    LinearisationLinéarisationMeasuring ink patches to set each ink's response on a media.
    ScreeningTramageTurning ink values into dots (4 drop sizes on the Epson).
    ReadingRelevéInk levels and roll read from the printer, for the real consumption.

    #Media and layout

    TermIn FrenchDefinition
    MediaSupportThe printed material (vinyl, banner…), with its roll width, inks and modes.
    Loaded mediaSupport chargéThe media in the machine, read from the Epson or chosen by the operator.
    Roll widthLaizeUsable width of the roll.
    BleedFond perduImage extended past the trim size, so a slightly off cut leaves no white.
    GrommetŒilletGrommet mark placed around the edge of a banner.
    TilingMosaïqueA large visual split into pieces that fit the roll width.
    PanelLéVertical strip of a tiled job, at most as wide as the roll.
    TileTuileOne piece of a tiling in columns × rows; each tile is a page.
    OverlapRecouvrementPart shared by two neighbouring panels, to join them when fitting.
    SheetPlancheSeveral jobs laid side by side on one page.
    NestingImbricationLaying jobs on a sheet, by hand or automatically in the queue.

    #Cutting

    TermIn FrenchDefinition
    PathTracéA line the cutter follows.
    Cut layerCouche de découpeThe paths of one spot colour name, cut together with one cut setting.
    Cut settingRéglage de coupeSpeed, force, acceleration, tool and pattern of a layer.
    RegistrationRepérageThe cutter reading printed marks, to cut exactly where the print is.
    Marks / FrameRepères / CadreCrosses or a frame (with a barcode for the Kona) printed around the image for registration.
    Auto contourDétourage automatiqueCut path computed around the image.
    Cutting toleranceTolérance de découpeInk extended past the cut contours, so a slightly off cut leaves no white edge.
    Bowing compensationCompensation de cintrageThe cut follows a print that comes out of the dryers curved (middle behind the edges).
    Offset X / YDécalage X / YMeasured gap between cut and print, added to every cut on a machine.
    Cut turned (180°)Découpe retournéeCut rotated by 180°, when the media goes in from the other end.
    VariantVarianteCopy of a cutter with its own set of marks.

    #Machines and server

    TermIn FrenchDefinition
    MachineMachinePrinter or cutter in the catalogue (System > Machines).
    ConnectionConnexionHow the server reaches the machine: TCP network, serial, device, folder, IPP or forwarding.
    Direct driverPilote directThe RIP builds the printer's data stream itself (the Epson's ESC/P2) and sends it.
    Folder connectionConnexion DossierNothing is sent: the output is copied into sorties/<machine>/.
    IPPIPPNetwork printing protocol (CUPS); the stream goes raw in a Print-Job.
    ForwardingRenvoiThe stream goes to another E-KO RIP Center, which passes it to its machine.
    TokenJetonSecret opening a machine-to-machine access (forwarding, ERP); never shown again.
    SpeedCadenceArea printed per hour (m²/h), to estimate times.
    SNMPSNMPReading the Epson's state over the network (media, inks, alerts).
    ERP bridgePont ERPSigned exchanges between the RIP and the E-KO ERP (eko-rip-erp/1 contract).
    Free space thresholdSeuil d'espace libreSpace to keep on the data disk; below it, purge and refusal of large jobs.
    ClientClientDesktop application (Mac, Windows, Linux) that opens the server's screen.
